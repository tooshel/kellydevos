Kelly deVos

contemporary & horror writer

↓

I write sharp, funny books about girls who refuse to shrink.

Five novels so far — a fashion-world debut, a doomsday duology, zombies at fat camp, and a Dracula-inspired castle slasher. My work on body positivity has appeared in the New York Times, Vulture, Salon, Bustle and SheKnows. I live in Gilbert, Arizona with my high school sweetheart, our daughter, and a superhero dog named Cocoa.
